Calendar of Events

NASA IRB and HRMRB/AHRCB meetings are closed to the public. Guests must be invited to the meetings by the board's respective Chair or their designee. All guests, as well as respective board members, are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of deliberations, discussions, and votes. Care should be taken to store and dispose of meeting materials and protocols should not be discussed with others outside of the meeting.
