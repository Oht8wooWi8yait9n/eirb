CONFLICT OF INTEREST CERTIFICATION NON-FEDERAL INVESTIGATORS

To protect the safety, rights, and welfare of research participants, promote research objectivity, and maintain the public’s trust, the NASA IRB must consider actual and perceived conflicts of interest that may arise during the conduct of human subjects research.

This form should be completed by individuals engaged in human subjects research who are not federal employees. Federal employees, who are subject to additional government-wide ethical conduct regulations and statutes i should complete ORA Form 5521. Individuals who are not federal employees are responsible for following their own institution/employer’s Conflict of Interest policies in addition to NASA policy. A new form must be promptly submitted to the NASA IRB whenever a new potential conflict is identified.

Please complete all the information below and retain a copy of this form for your records.
