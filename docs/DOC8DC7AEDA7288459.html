--- Page 1 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study Before you begin, please complete the NASA IRB NHSR Determination Questionnaire. For more information on “Not Human Subjects Research” Determination, please refer to SOP-4 05: Human Subjects Research Determination.

1. Visit https://eirb.jsc.nasa.gov/EIRB/ and click “Login” at the top right corner of the screen.

2. Enter your User Name and Password. Click “Login.”

3. Click “Dashboard” on the top menu bar.

--- Page 2 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study 4. Select “Create” in the left panel and then select “Create New Study.”

5. Basic Study Information - Complete sections 1 through 8. Note, all sections are required as indicated by a red asterisk (*). Since this is an NHSR project, a protocol is NOT required. Please upload the NASA NHSR Determination Questionnaire in place of the protocol.

When attaching the NHSR Determination Questionnaire, click “+ Add”. A new screen will appear. You will need to locate the file on your computer. Once the document has been uploaded, click “OK.”

--- Page 3 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study 6. Once you are finished completing sections 1 through 8, you may either save the information and return later to complete by clicking “Save” in the bottom left of the screen or continue with the smart form entry by clicking “Continue.”

--- Page 4 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study 7. Study Funding Sources a. This is NOT required to completed, if you do not have a funding source, simply select “Continue” at the bottom right of the screen and skip Step 8. b. I f you do have a funding source, you may enter the Study Funding Source information by selecting “+ Add.” A new screen will appear.

8. Once selected, you will be prompted in the new screen to select the Funding Organization (a required entry). Click the three dots shown below to select a funding source. This is a progressive search. Start typing the name of the organization and you will be presented with a list to pick from. If your funding source does not appear, select “other” from the list. Once complete, you may either add an additional funding source or exit the screen to continue the protocol entry by clicking “OK.” Then, click “Continue” at the bottom right of the screen.

--- Page 5 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study

9. Local Study Team Members – For an NHSR determination, you are NOT required to enter Local Study Team Members or External Team Member Information. This also applies to CITI training certificates and Financial Conflict of Interest Disclosure forms. Click “Continue” at the bottom right of the screen.

10. Study Scope – Since this is an NHSR submission, questions 1 and 2 should be “No”. Once completed, click “Continue” at the bottom right of the screen. a. If you selected “yes” to question 1 and/or question 2, the Drugs and/or Devices section will appear on the smart form, and the study is likely human subjects research.

--- Page 6 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study 11. Local Research Locations – For an NHSR determination, you are NOT required to add a research location. Select “Continue” at the bottom right of the screen to proceed.

12. Local Site Documents – In this section, consent forms and recruitment materials are NOT required for an NHSR determination.

Data collection materials (e.g., surveys, questionnaires) and other relevant study materials are also NOT required as part of an NHSR determination, but they may be requested by IRB staff when making the determination. Note: If your project involves secondary data obtained from LSDA/LSAH, LSDA/LSAH documentation will be required.

To upload any data collection materials or documentation, simply click on the “+ Add” button in the Other Attachments area. A new window will open (please see guidance provided in #5 of this document) and you will locate the files on your computer to upload. Once finished, click “OK” in the bottom right of the new screen and continue with your smart form entry.

--- Page 7 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study

13. Additional Information – Answer all 5 required questions. Click “Continue” in the bottom right corner of the screen when completed.

--- Page 8 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study 14. Final Page – If ready to submit, click “Finish.”

15. You will see the screen below indicating the Study is now in the “Pre-Submission” phase. The study has not been submitted to the IRB.

--- Page 9 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study 16. You may adjust the smart form entries in this stage before submitting. When ready to submit to the IRB office, click “Submit” on the left side of the screen.

17. Once selected, a new screen will open as shown below. Click “OK” to verify.

18. An additional pop-up window will appear. Enter your e-IRB username and password, then click “submit.”

--- Page 10 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study

19. When successfully submitted to the IRB, the study will be in the “Pre-Review” state as shown below.

--- Page 11 --- NASA IRB Huron Guide for Researchers How to Submit a New “Not Human Subjects Research” (NHSR) Study Checklist for Information to Attach (NHSR)

Attach the information listed below (if relevant to your study) to the location identified. Please remember that additional documentation may be requested by the IRB Coordinator reviewing the submission.

Protocol: (Basic Information page) • NASA NHSR Determination Questionnaire

Funding Information: (Funding Sources page, with each source) • Not Required

Drug details: (Drugs page, with each drug, or on main Drugs page if not specific to one drug) • Not Required

Device details: (Devices page, with each device, or on main Devices page if not specific to one device) • Not Required

Recruitment and consent details: (Local Site Documents page) • Not Required

All other relevant documents: (Study-Related Documents page) • LSDA/LSAH Documentation (projects involving secondary data obtained from LSDA or LSAH) • Per request by IRB Coordinator
