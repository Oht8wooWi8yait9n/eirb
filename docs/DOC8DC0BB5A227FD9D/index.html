NASA INSTITUTIONAL REVIEW BOARD (IRB)

CONSENT TO BE A PART OF A RESEARCH STUDY

ABOUT THIS RESEARCH CONSENT FORM

You may be eligible to take part in a research study.

Please take time to review this information carefully. Talk to the researchers about the study and ask any questions you have. Make sure you fully understand what will be expected of you and the risks associated with participating in this study.

Taking part in this study is completely voluntary. The decision to participate is yours. You may also leave the study at any time. If you leave the study before it is finished, there will be no penalty to you.

1. GENERAL INFORMATION

Study Title: [Please insert study title here]

The study team includes: [Please insert names/roles/degrees/affiliations here]

2. STUDY PROCEDURES

In this section provide an easily understandable study description including (when applicable):

study procedures and briefly describe the information that will be collected from those procedures

amount of time required

location(s)

other pertinent information to assist an individual in making an informed decision about participation in the study.

When applicable, include:

any use of information or data from records

plans for audio/video recordings or photographs

plans for capturing info via screen-recording, key-stroke-logging

If deception about the nature or purpose of the study is planned, prospective subjects must be informed that they will be unaware of, or misled about, the nature or purposes of the research and agree to proceed.

3. Risks

  • [Describe potential risks/discomfort and describe steps you are taking to minimize/mitigate these risks]
  • For example, if risks are related to psychological discomforts related to sensitive or personal questions:
  • The [interview/survey] includes questions that you may find sensitive, uncomfortable, or difficult to answer. You may skip questions or stop at any time.

4. PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

Your privacy and the confidentiality of your information will be protected in the following ways:

[Describe privacy and confidentiality measures include information about future data use/sharing]

For example:

Any identifiers collected as part of this study will be promptly removed from your information and replaced with a code that is stored separate and securely from the data. Only the research team will have access to the key the linking study data to your name.

Study data that have been stripped of any information that could identify you (de-identified) may be made public or used for future research purposes.

  • Audio/video recordings will be destroyed promptly following transcription of the interview and will never be shared with others or used beyond this project. We may share or use the text transcript of your interview for future research purposes; however, we will take steps to remove any information that may identify you before the transcript is shared.
  • You will have the opportunity to review this transcript and request revisions or retractions. When we write-up our findings from the study, we will replace your name with a pseudonym (fake name) and will take care to change details that could indirectly identify you or others

If you have any questions you may contact the Principal Investigator, [insert PI name], at [insert PI contact information] or the NASA Institutional Review Board (IRB) at:
